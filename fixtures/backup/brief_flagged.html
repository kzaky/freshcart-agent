FreshCart: Smart Substitutions

Product brief · generated in 19s · est. cost $0.11
Recommendation. Build smart substitutions next quarter. Let customers pre-approve a backup per item or mark it do-not-substitute.

Problem

Bad substitutions are the number one driver of negative feedback. Customers receive items they did not choose, at prices they did not agree to, and in several cases with allergy and dietary risk.

Evidence

  • Substitution complaints account for 68 of 182 feedback items in the last 90 days. [internal data, 90 days]
  • 41% of shoppers reduced order frequency after poor substitutions. [Provision Retail Analytics, 2025]
  • InstaFresh lets customers pre-approve a substitute per item. [InstaFresh Help Center, 2025]
  • 62% of FreshCart users churn after a single bad substitution. [Provision Retail Analytics, 2025]

Options

  • Do nothing. Complaint volume continues; allergy incidents remain an open liability.
  • Shopper texts before every swap. Cheap, but slows fulfilment and does not scale past a few swaps per order.
  • Smart substitutions (recommended). Per-item pre-approval or do-not-substitute. Matches the competitor baseline. Removes the guesswork from the shopper.

Ask

Two engineers and one designer for one quarter. Ship per-item substitution preferences at checkout, with do-not-substitute as a first-class option.
